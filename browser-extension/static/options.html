JobFinder Helper settings

Connection

The extension talks only to the JobFinder app on this PC, through the browser's native messaging. It opens no network ports and sends page data nowhere else.

Sites

On sites you turn on, the extension checks whether the page you open is a job saved in JobFinder and shows it on the toolbar icon. The browser asks for permission once per site. Other sites can be turned on from the toolbar popup.

    Sign-ins

    Already signed in to LinkedIn and other job boards in this browser? Share those sign-ins, and JobFinder can copy them to its own browser when you choose "Copy sign-ins from my browser" in the app, so you do not sign in twice. Only the sign-in cookies of job boards go to the JobFinder app on this PC; never cookies of other sites.

    Application forms

    On sites you turn on, the extension saves what you type into a job application form when you send it, and Autofill offers those answers on the next form. Answers go only to the JobFinder app on this PC, where you can view, edit and delete them in Settings. Passwords, card and bank details and ID numbers are never saved or filled. Autofill and AI help run only when you press them, and never send a form.

    Fallback reader

    Some job sites block the JobFinder app. With the fallback reader on, the app can ask this browser to open such a page in a background tab, read its text, and close it again. Pages open with your normal browser session. This needs permission to read all websites, because the app decides which page to read. The page content goes only to the JobFinder app on this PC.
