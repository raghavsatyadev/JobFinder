# Privacy

- No account, telemetry or server of ours.
- Your data stays in one folder on your computer: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\jobfinder-data`
  (`~/.jobfinder-data` on Linux).
- Job boards see the same requests your own browser makes when you search.
- The phone talks to the computer only on your local network, after you allow it there.
- Online AI is off until you add a key. Keys stay on the computer, protected by Windows, and go
  only into locked backups made on the computer.
- The browser extension talks only to the app on the same computer and never sends a form.

Full policy: <https://raghavsatyadev.github.io/projects/jobfinder/privacy/>
