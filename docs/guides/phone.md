# Phone

The Android app is a window into the desktop app: it shows your jobs, lets you archive, star and
apply, edits your AI filter, and tells you about new jobs while the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as
the computer. Away from home it shows what it saw last, and your changes reach the computer when
you are back.

Nothing goes through the internet: the phone talks to the computer on your home network only, with
a key made when you paired.

## Pair it

1. Install `JobFinder-<version>.apk` from the
   [Releases](https://github.com/raghavsatyadev/JobFinder/releases) page (Android 8.0 or newer).
2. Put the phone on the computer's Wi-Fi and turn off any VPN. Guest networks keep devices apart.
3. On the computer, the setup's phone step sets your Wi-Fi to Private and opens the firewall to
   your own network only. Windows asks once; choose Yes.
4. Open JobFinder on the phone. Your computer shows up in the list; tap it. Not listed? Scan the
   code in the desktop app's Settings → Phone pairing.
5. The computer asks whether to allow the phone. Click **Allow** when both show the same code.

## Not connecting

- Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network, and that no VPN is on.
- Use **Check the connection** in the phone's Help.
- A new computer has a new pairing code: add the computer again on the phone.
