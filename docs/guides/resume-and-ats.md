# Resume and ATS

Upload your resume in **Settings → Resume** (PDF, DOCX or TXT). The app reads it and flags what
ATS software trips on: tables, several columns, images, icon fonts, missing Experience, Skills or
Education headings, roles without dates, no email or phone, too short or too long.

## The ATS review

For each job in New the AI writes an ATS review against your resume:

- An overall score and the keyword match: each keyword found exactly, as a variant, or missing.
- How relevant your experience is, the quality of your achievements, education fit and career
  path.
- What would get you screened out, an assessment, and the top fixes.

Sort the jobs by **Highest ATS score** to see the best matches first.

## Improve my resume for this job

Under a ready review, **Improve my resume for this job** writes exact before and after edits that
bring in the job's words. It keeps every fact and number true; an edit that adds a skill or number
your resume lacks is flagged, so you add it only if it is true.

Tick the edits you want, see the new match score, then copy the new text, save it, or **Save as
PDF** for a resume PDF fitted to this job with those edits.

## Resume PDFs

**Settings → Resume → Resume PDF** makes a one-column PDF that ATS software reads well:

- **Page size** A4 or Letter, and **Length** 1 or 2 pages. When it runs long, the bullets least
  related to the job are left out of the oldest roles until it fits.
- **Sections, in order**: tick the sections to show and move them up or down.
- **Save resume PDF** makes the general PDF from your resume.

A PDF for a job (Save as PDF in "Improve my resume for this job") puts the skills and bullets with
the job's words first. With your LinkedIn data imported and **Bring in LinkedIn content for a job**
on, it also brings in your own LinkedIn skills, role bullets (matched to the role by company
name), projects and certifications that have the words the job asks for and your resume lacks.
Nothing is written by AI or made up. Each PDF is read back like an ATS reads it, and the app shows
its match next to your resume's.

## LinkedIn data

LinkedIn has your full history; a resume has room for part of it. To import it:

1. On a computer, open LinkedIn → **Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your
   data** (linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data).
2. Choose **Want something in particular?** and tick Profile, Positions, Education, Skills,
   Certifications, Projects, Languages, Honors and Courses.
3. **Request archive.** LinkedIn emails a link in about 10 minutes; it works for 72 hours.
4. Download the ZIP and import it with **Import LinkedIn data**, as it is; don't unzip it.

Only the profile, roles, education, skills, certifications, projects, languages, honors, courses,
email and phone are read. Messages, connections and everything else in the ZIP are never read, and
the ZIP is not kept. Import again any time your profile changes; **Remove** deletes it. Skills
in your LinkedIn data are not flagged as new in the resume edits. The LinkedIn data and PDF
choices are part of the Resume settings in backups.

## A good resume for ATS

- One column; standard headings (Summary, Experience, Skills, Education).
- Company, title and dates for every role.
- Short bullets that start with a verb and show results with numbers.
- The target roles' keywords, only where they are true.
- No tables, images, icons or text boxes; one or two pages.

During setup, the app can copy a request for an AI assistant you already use (Claude, Gemini,
ChatGPT) to rewrite your resume from what the ATS check found: attach your resume there and paste
the answer back.
