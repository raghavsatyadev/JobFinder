# Resume and ATS

Upload your resume in **Settings → Resume** (PDF, DOCX or TXT). The app reads it and flags what
ATS software trips on: tables, several columns, images, icon fonts, missing Experience, Skills or
Education headings, roles without dates, no email or phone, too short or too long.

## The ATS review

For each job in New the AI writes an ATS review against your resume:

- An overall score and the keyword match: each keyword found exactly, as a variant, or missing.
- How relevant your experience is, the quality of your achievements, education fit and career
  path.
- What would get you screened out, an assessment, and the top fixes.

Sort the jobs by **Highest ATS score** to see the best matches first.

## Improve my resume for this job

Under a ready review, **Improve my resume for this job** writes exact before and after edits that
bring in the job's words. It keeps every fact and number true; an edit that adds a skill or number
your resume lacks is flagged, so you add it only if it is true.

Tick the edits you want, see the new match score, then copy the new text or save it.

## A good resume for ATS

- One column; standard headings (Summary, Experience, Skills, Education).
- Company, title and dates for every role.
- Short bullets that start with a verb and show results with numbers.
- The target roles' keywords, only where they are true.
- No tables, images, icons or text boxes; one or two pages.

During setup, the app can copy a request for an AI assistant you already use (Claude, Gemini,
ChatGPT) to rewrite your resume from what the ATS check found: attach your resume there and paste
the answer back.
