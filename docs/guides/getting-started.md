# Get started

JobFinder runs on your Windows computer (Linux is a preview). It checks your job searches, keeps
every new job, lets an AI filter out the ones you do not want and scores your resume against the
rest. The Android app shows the same jobs on your phone over your home Wi-Fi.

## 1. Install

Download the latest release from the
[Releases](https://github.com/raghavsatyadev/JobFinder/releases) page and run
`JobFinder-<version>.msi`. If Windows warns that the publisher is unknown, choose More info, then
Run anyway. Open JobFinder from the Start menu.

## 2. Follow the setup

The first time, the app opens a short setup. Each step checks itself, and you can keep using the
app while things download.

1. **The AI.** One button installs [Ollama](https://ollama.com) and downloads a small local model
   in the background. On a slow computer, add free online keys too: see [Free AI](free-ai.md).
2. **Your resume.** Settings → Resume. ATS scores, tips and resume fixes use it: see
   [Resume and ATS](resume-and-ats.md).
3. **Searches.** Add 10 to 15 search result pages: see [Job searches](job-searches.md).
4. **Sign in to the job sites.** Open sign-in opens the sites your searches use, one tab each.
   Sign in on every tab, then close that browser. Signed-in searches show every job.
5. **The AI filter.** Describe the jobs to skip: see [AI filter](ai-filter.md).
6. **Your phone (optional).** See [Phone](phone.md).

## 3. Use it

- New jobs arrive in **New**. Jobs the AI filter skipped go to **Filtered out** with the reason.
- Open a job to read it, see its ATS review, company details and ghost-job check, then apply on
  the job site. Move it to **Applied** or **Archived**, or star it.
- Sort by date or ATS score, and filter by board, search, label and work mode.
- Keep the app open while you look for a job. Settings → Scanning sets how often it searches
  (every 12 hours is the default) and can keep the computer awake.

Stuck? Press the question mark in the app for step-by-step guides and common questions, or let an
AI agent help: see [AI agents](ai-agents.md).
