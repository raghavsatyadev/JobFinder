# Backups and moving computers

**Settings → Backup and move → Export everything** saves one file with your searches, jobs, notes,
ATS reviews and settings. On the computer it holds your online API keys too. The file is always
locked:

- With a password you choose, it opens on any computer or phone.
- Without a password, only the device that saved it can open it.

The computer also saves a locked backup every day and keeps the last 7. A CSV export of your jobs
opens in any spreadsheet app.

## Move to a new computer

1. Export everything with a password, and keep the file and the password safe (a USB drive or a
   cloud folder).
2. Install JobFinder on the new computer and finish the setup.
3. Settings → **Import a backup**, pick the file, type its password and choose **Replace
   everything**. On a computer you already use, choose **Add what's missing** instead.
4. Sign in to the job sites again, and add the new computer on your phone: sign-ins and pairing
   codes are never in backups.

The phone can open a backup too. Its jobs show on the phone at once and reach the computer when it
is in reach; replacing everything from the phone needs your yes on the computer.
