## Full description (≤ 4000)

JobFinder for Android is the phone companion of the free JobFinder app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Your computer checks your saved job board searches and finds new jobs as soon as they are posted; your phone shows them to you wherever you are in the house, and tells you when new ones arrive.

There is no account and no server. The phone talks only to your own computer, over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet.

SEE NEW JOBS FIRST
Get a notification when your computer finds new jobs, so you can be one of the first to apply. Sort by date or ATS score, and filter by board, search, label and work mode.

KNOW EACH JOB BEFORE YOU APPLY
Open a job to read the description, the ATS review your computer wrote for your resume (match score, keywords you have and miss, what could screen you out, top fixes), and why a job was filtered out.

KEEP YOUR LIST TIDY
Star, archive or delete jobs, and the change reaches your computer too.

CHECK YOUR SETUP
Look at your saved searches, AI filters and settings from your phone.

PAIR IN SECONDS
Keep JobFinder open on your computer, pick it from the list on your phone (or scan its pairing code), and click Allow on the computer. Pair more than one computer if you like.

NO COMPUTER YET?
Tap "Try with sample jobs" on the pairing screen to look around with sample data. Nothing in sample mode is real, and nothing leaves your phone.

JobFinder does not apply to jobs for you. It finds them early; you apply yourself.

Needs the JobFinder desktop app (free) on a computer on the same Wi-Fi network.
